
## Vacuum gripper harware issues

### Problem: Arm extension can come loose

The extension arm may become imprecise (about 5mm) depending on the direction it comes from.

.VGR with gap on left or right 
[#img-vgr-arm-extesion-with-gap]
[cols="a,a"]
|===
| image::./img/VacuumGripper/VGR_arm_extension_gap_on_left.jpg[float="left",align="center", 240]
| image::./img/VacuumGripper/VGR_arm_extension_gap_on_right.jpg[float="left",align="center", 240]
|===


This is due to a gap that may appear when extending the arm too far outwards. (see the 2 figures <<img-vgr-arm-extesion-with-gap>>)




.VGR without arm exension gap 
[#img-vgr-arm-extension-without-gap]
image::./img/VacuumGripper/VGR_arm-extension-without-gap.jpg[float="left",align="center", 240]

**Fix:** by adjusting the screw at the base of the axis. (see <<img-vgr-arm-extension-without-gap>>)

Idea: use some soft glue to prevent this gap
