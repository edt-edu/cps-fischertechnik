
## Conveyor belt hardware issues

### Problem: Conveyor belt can get stuck

The Conveyor belt can collide with the black axle at the one end of the conveyor when the black axle is mounted in the wrong way.

.CB axle mounted wrong
[#img-cb-axle-mounted-wrong]
[cols="a,a"]
|===
| image::./img/ConveyorBelt/CB_axle_mounted_wrong.jpg[float="left",align="center", 240]
| image::./img/ConveyorBelt/CB_axle_mounted_wrong2.jpg[float="left",align="center", 240]
|===

Out of the box the axle should be mounted correctly but when it falls of and is reattatched this mistake can happen.

**Fix:** watch out that the two red parts of the mounting blocks are pointing downwards. (see <<img-cb-axle-mounted-correctly>>)
